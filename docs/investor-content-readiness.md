# Investor content readiness

The public investment and partnerships page supports introductions only. It is not a business valuation, funding offer or investor presentation. It uses owner-provided hospitality services and actual supplied grounds photos. No numbers, returns, ownership history, development approvals or sustainability certifications have been invented.

## Information owners need to prepare

- Legal business name, registration details, ownership and authorised investment contact.
- Verified land tenure and boundaries, licences and relevant approvals.
- Actual accommodation inventory, operating capacity and current service facilities.
- Historical accounts and evidence for any revenue, occupancy or bookings claims.
- Specific funding objective, use of funds, proposed structure and milestones approved by owners.
- Supported development budget and assumptions; clear distinction between existing operations and proposed expansion.
- Current property photos, site plan and any approved expansion drawings.
- Appropriate process for sharing sensitive documents privately with prospective investors.

Keep documents containing personal, financial, title or commercially sensitive information out of the public website and public repository. The introduction form does not request documents, payment or financial commitments, and does not save personal details. It prepares a WhatsApp message which the visitor reviews and sends.

## Shipped website content

- About page presenting the six owner-confirmed services, location context and international contact guidance.
- Investment and partnerships page with factual overview, enquiry categories, discussion steps and a dedicated WhatsApp introduction form.
- Printable resort overview, suitable for browser print / save as PDF.
- Shared utility navigation and footer access; existing guest booking paths retained.
