# Joy Ripples hospitality website research

Reviewed 24 November 2026. This is a qualitative review of four official hotel websites, not a ranking of all hotels or a conversion study.

| Official source | Observed pattern | Applied to Joy Ripples |
|---|---|---|
| [Hemingways Nairobi dining](https://www.hemingways-collection.com/nairobi/dining/) | Separate dining occasions with menu and reservation links | Clear dining enquiry route alongside the menu |
| [Tribe / Jiko](https://www.tribe-hotel.com/nairobi/jiko-restaurant-nairobi/) | Distinct restaurant identity, concise introduction and direct enquiry | Stronger food-led introduction within Joy Ripples branding |
| [Sarova catering](https://www.sarovahotels.com/catering.html) | Dedicated catering experience, event types and enquiry links | Separate catering page, occasion selection and detailed event brief |
| [Four Seasons Hong Kong / The Lounge](https://www.fourseasons.com/hongkong/dining/restaurants/the_lounge/) | Menus, practical information and reservations grouped together | Adjacent menu, catering and dining enquiry links |

## Implementation decisions

Keep the approved cream, forest green and copper palette. Use supplied resort photographs on the catering page. Preserve cart-based browsing, variants and local saved selections. Keep the cart available while browsing. Provide a catering calculator that distinguishes an indicative food subtotal from a confirmed quotation and collects date, serving time, venue, guest count, service preferences and dietary requests.

Benchmark rates and proposed menu items remain unconfirmed. Do not import other hotels' amenities, menus, hours, awards, capacities, terms or operational promises into Joy Ripples. No money is collected; WhatsApp enquiries are reviewed and sent by the visitor.

## Information needed for an operational launch

- Resort-approved dishes, portions, prices and availability.
- Catering inclusions, service areas, minimums and fees.
- Confirmed opening hours, location and directions.
- Approved booking, deposit and cancellation terms.
- Room photographs and verified accommodation details.

These factual gaps cannot be solved through competitor design research. The resort must supply or approve them.
